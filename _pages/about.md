Hi! I am a Research Fellow at the [National University of Singapore (NUS)](https://www.nus.edu.sg/). I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from NUS, supported by the [President's Graduate Fellowship](https://nusgs.nus.edu.sg/scholarships/presidents-graduate-fellowship). Prior to this, I earned my Bachelor's degree in Aerospace Engineering from [Nanyang Technological University (NTU)](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/), where I was part of the [C. N. Yang Scholars Programme](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/honours-college/signature-programmes/cn-yang-scholars-programme) and a recipient of the ASEAN Undergraduate Scholarship. 

My research focuses on understanding the physical mechanisms governing materials processing and translating these insights into more reliable and sustainable manufacturing strategies, with particular emphasis on metal additive manufacturing. During my Ph.D., I combined multiphysics simulations and experimental approaches to elucidate gas–liquid metal interactions, focusing on their implications for material transformations, oxygen reactions, and metal powder reuse. My research also explores AI-assisted reconstruction of subsurface melt pool geometries from in situ images to advance process monitoring capabilities. In addition, I leverage my understanding of melt pool physics to contribute to experimental alloy development for laser powder bed fusion processes.

My ongoing research builds on these foundations while exploring broader questions in materials processing, process–material interactions, and advanced manufacturing.
